01 — Apps

What's live

One tool that runs entirely in your browser, and three self-hosted apps for tasks, money and notes. Each is small, focused, and built to be used every day.

Live

HideIt

Paste a log, config or command and get it back with emails, passwords, tokens and IDs swapped for random values. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

Open →
Live

Tasks

A Kanban board for personal and work tasks. Cards move from To Do to Done, carry an Eisenhower priority, and take categories and tags. Works on desktop and phone.

Open →
Live

Spending

A mobile-first income and expense tracker. Multiple accounts and currencies, transfers between them, a monthly budget, and reports that drill down by date or category.

Docs

A personal knowledge base. Notes that mix text, images, flowcharts and drawings, organised by category and tag, fully searchable, with version history.
